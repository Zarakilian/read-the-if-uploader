Read the If uploader

A private desktop tool for the owner of the Read the If YouTube channel.

The tool connects the channel owner's Google account to a local production workflow. The connection verifies the channel identity. The planned upload workflow will send owner-approved videos and their metadata to YouTube, with publication times chosen by the owner.

Owner-controlled access

This is a personal channel tool, not a public sign-up service. Google authorization requests permission to upload videos and read YouTube account information. Every upload or scheduling task requires an instruction from the channel owner. Connecting the account does not itself upload or publish a video.

OAuth credentials stay in an encrypted local Windows store. This website does not accept credentials or provide a login form.
